--- layout: home author_profile: false --- Bible By Example is a long-form, text-first Bible study podcast for anyone who wants to read Scripture responsibly - whether you’re new to Christianity, returning to it, or simply curious. I’m not a pastor or a scholar; I’m a fellow traveler walking through the Bible chapter by chapter, focusing on what the text actually says. Along the way, I highlight where major Christian traditions differ, explain translation choices, and occasionally dive into language or history when it genuinely helps. If you want grounded, thoughtful study without denominational spin, this is your place.
