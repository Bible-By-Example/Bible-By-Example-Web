## How Your Support Is Used

I believe supporters deserve to know where their money goes. While I won't be posting quarterly accounting statements, here's a general overview of how support for *Bible By Example* is handled behind the scenes. These priorities may change over time as this project grows, but this is the general approach I'll continue to follow.

### Tithes

The first 10% of every dollar received is set aside as a tithe. I don't expect everyone to share my faith, but I do want everyone who supports this project to know where that portion goes.

The tithe currently goes to [Wells of Living Water](https://www.wellsoflivingwater.org/), a ministry I know personally and am happy to support. Their work serves communities in Peru by drilling wells, providing access to clean drinking water, and sharing the Gospel.

### Operating Costs and Infrastructure

After the tithe, support helps cover the ordinary costs of producing and distributing the show, including:

- Federal, state, and local taxes.
- Domain registration.
- Website and podcast hosting.
- Business software and administrative services.
- Payment processing fees.
- Equipment maintenance and replacement.

### Reinvestment

Whatever remains after tithes, taxes, and operating expenses is reinvested into the project &mdash; funding research materials, upgrading equipment, and helping offset the time spent writing, recording, and producing *Bible By Example*.

As the project grows, future support will help:

- Purchase additional study resources and reference works.
- Improve recording equipment and production quality.
- Upgrade the RSS.com hosting plan.
- Expand the Google Workspace subscription.
- Cover hosting and service costs as the project outgrows today's free tiers.
