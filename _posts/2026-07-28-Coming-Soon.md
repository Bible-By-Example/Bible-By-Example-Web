<!--more-->

Bible By Example is a long-form, text-first Bible study podcast for anyone who wants to read Scripture responsibly — whether you’re a new Christian, a lifelong churchgoer, or an agnostic who wants to understand the text accurately.

I’m not a pastor or scholar; I’m a fellow traveler walking through the Bible chapter by chapter, focusing on what the text actually says. We go strictly in order from Genesis to Revelation and never cross-reference forward, so we can see the context unfold in real time. Along the way, I’ll map out where major traditions differ, share history and language insights, and openly invite you to check my work.

If you want a thoughtful study without denominational spin, this is your place.
